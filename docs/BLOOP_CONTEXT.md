# Master AI Context Profile: bloop (Product & Growth Edition)

## 1. Company Profile & Core Concept

- **Company Name:** bloop

- **Stage:** Early-stage Startup (Launched 6 months ago)

- **HQ Location:** Portugal (Expanding to Spain)

- **Industry:** Social Commerce, E-commerce Marketplace, FinTech

### The Product Definition

bloop is a **social shopping network** that natively fuses a discoverable social media feed with a frictionless e-commerce marketplace. It eliminates the friction between inspiration (seeing a product on social media) and transaction (buying it on an e-commerce platform).

Instead of professional influencers dominating the economics, bloop's architecture treats **every everyday consumer as an incentivized micro-influencer**.

---

## 2. The Core Product Loop: "Credit-Back" UX Flow

The heart of bloop's retention and viral loop is an automated, integrated financial incentive mechanism built directly into the post-purchase user flow:

- **Step 1: The Purchase:** A user completes a native checkout for any item on the marketplace.

- **Step 2: The Social Trigger:** Immediately post-purchase, the UI prompts the user to share a photo/video post of their purchase to the native bloop feed.

- **Step 3: Base Reward:** Publishing this post automatically unlocks **up to 10% credit-back** (**5% for tech categories**) applied to their native wallet for their next purchase.

- **Step 4: The Viral Multiplier:** Every post acts as a shoppable portal. If another user taps the post and buys the exact same item, the original creator instantly gets _another_ 10% (or 5%) credit-back. This is completely uncapped. If 10 people buy through their post, their original item becomes effectively free.
